Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Who we are

Listo IA ("we", "us") creates educational short-form videos about AI tools. Contact: holalistoia@gmail.com.

What our app does

Our internal publishing app connects only to accounts owned by Listo IA on Instagram, YouTube and TikTok. It uploads our own original videos and captions, and reads aggregate performance metrics of those videos (for example views, likes, comments, shares and watch time) so we can improve our content.

Information we access

  • Our own account data provided by each platform's official API: account ID, username, our published media and their metrics.
  • Public comments on our own posts, if and when we enable comment management, used only to read and reply to them.
  • Email addresses only if a person voluntarily subscribes to receive a free resource, with explicit consent and a way to unsubscribe at any time.

We do not access private data of other users, we do not sell personal data, and we do not use data for advertising profiles.

How we store and protect data

Access tokens and data are stored locally on our own computer, are never shared with third parties, and are used only for the purposes above. We keep metrics only as long as they are useful for content analysis.

Google and YouTube

Our use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. We comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can revoke our access at any time from Google security settings.

TikTok and Meta

Data received from TikTok and Meta (Instagram/Facebook) APIs is used only as described here and in accordance with their developer terms. Access can be revoked from each platform's app settings.

Your rights and data deletion

You can ask us to access or delete any personal data related to you (for example, your email address or a comment we stored) by writing to holalistoia@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Children

Our content and services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and revise the date above.
